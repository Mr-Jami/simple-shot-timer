Hosting and server logs

This site is hosted by Netlify (Netlify, Inc., 512 2nd Street, Suite 200, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you open a page, Netlify processes technically necessary access data as a processor on my behalf:

  • the truncated or full IP address of the requesting device
  • date and time of the request
  • name and URL of the file retrieved, and the volume of data transferred
  • browser and operating system used
  • the previously visited page, where sent (referrer)

This processing is technically required to deliver the site, keep it stable and defend against attacks. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; my legitimate interest lies in operating the site securely and reliably. A data processing agreement under Art. 28 GDPR is in place with Netlify.

As Netlify is a US company, data may be transferred to the United States. That transfer is based on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses under Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR; Netlify is additionally certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Despite these safeguards, access by US authorities cannot be ruled out.

Fonts

All fonts are served locally from this server. There is no connection to Google Fonts or any other external font service, so no data is transmitted to third parties.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code and loads no content from third parties.

The timer demo

The timer at the top of the homepage runs entirely in your browser. If you switch on "Clap to fire", your browser asks for permission to use the microphone. While that option is on, the signal is analysed in your browser's memory to spot claps. Nothing is recorded, saved or sent to me, to Netlify or to anyone else. The legal basis is your consent through the browser's permission prompt (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR); you can switch the option off at any time or revoke the permission in your browser's settings.

Links to other sites

Links to Google Play, GitHub and jami-it.de are ordinary links. No data goes to those services until you follow one; from then on, their own privacy policies apply.

Contact by e-mail

If you contact me directly by e-mail, I process your details in order to deal with your request. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR. Transport encryption is normally used; end-to-end encryption cannot be guaranteed.

Retention

Server logs are deleted after 30 days at the latest. Enquiries by e-mail are deleted once they have been dealt with, unless statutory retention obligations apply — for business correspondence that can be six or ten years under §§ 147 AO, 257 HGB.

Your rights
