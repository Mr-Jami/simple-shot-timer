[AlternativeTo](https://alternativeto.net/) lists apps as alternatives to each
other; people (and AI assistants) use it to answer "what else is like X?".

[Shot Session](https://alternativeto.net/software/shot-session/about/), a

that page as the free, open-source, cross-platform option.

## Adding it

1. Create an account on AlternativeTo and choose *Add new app* (in the menu
   behind your profile, or <https://alternativeto.net/add-app/>).
2. Fill in the fields below. Developers may list their own apps; keep the
   text factual, as below (AlternativeTo's moderators remove marketing copy).
3. After it is published, open the Shot Session page, choose *Suggest
   alternative* and pick Simple Shot Timer (and the other way round on our
   page), so both pages link to each other.
4. When F-Droid publishes the app, add the F-Droid link.

New listings go through moderation, usually within a day or two.

**Description** ("What is Simple Shot Timer?"):

```text
Simple Shot Timer turns a phone into a shot timer for practical shooting (IPSC, USPSA and similar) and dry fire. It gives a start beep after an instant, fixed or random delay, detects every shot through the microphone and shows the time of each shot and the splits between them.

It has Standard, Par and Stage drill modes, saved drills, auto-configuration of the shot detection for a gun and range, a microphone test with a live level meter, a history of every string with review, notes and penalties, and CSV export. The clock starts when the microphone hears the start beep, so speaker latency doesn't end up in the times, and the timer keeps running with the screen locked.

Everything stays on the device: no account, no ads, no analytics. The app is open source under GPL-3.0 and available in English, German, Spanish, Finnish, French, Polish, Portuguese and Russian.
```
