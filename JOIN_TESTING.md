# Join the Simple Shot Timer beta

Simple Shot Timer is available to everyone on
[Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=cc.jami.simpleshottimer).
Beta testers get new builds before they reach the public release and help us
catch issues that don't show up on the bench.

## How to join (Android)

You'll need an Android phone and a Google account (the same one you use on
the Play Store).

### 1. Join the tester group

Open this link and click **Join group**:

**https://groups.google.com/d/forum/simple-shot-timer**

This is what tells Google's systems that your account is allowed to download
beta builds. The group is open to anyone, no approval needed. You can leave
any time.

### 2. Opt in to the beta

While signed in to the **same** Google account, open the store listing (it
works on your phone or any browser):

**https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=cc.jami.simpleshottimer**

Tap **Become a tester**.

### 3. Install or update

If you don't have the app yet, tap **Install** as you would for any other
app. If you already have it, the beta arrives as a normal update from the
Play Store. Your history and saved drills stay on the phone.

> If the Play Store doesn't offer the beta yet, give it 5–10 minutes after
> joining the group: there's a sync delay between Google's systems. Make sure
> you're signed in to the same account you used to join the group.

## Leaving the beta

Open the store listing again and tap **Leave the program**. You keep the beta
build until the next public release replaces it.

Uninstalling and reinstalling gets you the public build straight away, but
it deletes your history and saved drills: they are stored only on the phone.

## Reporting bugs and feedback

Create a [GitHub Issue](https://github.com/Mr-Jami/simple-shot-timer/issues).
Please include your phone model, Android version and the app version
(Settings, bottom of the page) so we can reproduce the issue.
