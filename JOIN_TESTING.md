# Join the closed test for Simple Shot Timer

Simple Shot Timer is currently in **closed testing** on the Google Play Store.
Testers get new builds before the public release and help us catch issues
that don't show up on the bench.

## How to join (Android)

You'll need an Android phone and a Google account (the same one you use on
the Play Store).

### 1. Join the tester group

Open this link and click **Join group**:

**https://groups.google.com/d/forum/simple-shot-timer**

This is what tells Google's systems that your account is allowed to download
the test build. The group is open to anyone — no approval needed. You can
leave any time.

### 2. Opt in to the test

While signed in to the **same** Google account, open this link (it works on
your phone or any browser):

**https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=cc.jami.simpleshottimer**

Tap **Become a tester**, then **Download it on Google Play**.

### 3. Install

The Play Store will open the Simple Shot Timer listing. Tap **Install** as
you would for any other app.

> If the Play Store says the app is unavailable or you can't find it, give
> it 5–10 minutes after joining the group — there's a sync delay between
> Google's systems. Make sure you're signed in to the same account you used
> to join the group.

## Leaving the test

- Open the opt-in link again and tap **Leave the program**, or
- Uninstall the app.

## Reporting bugs and feedback

Create a GitHub Issue. Please include your phone model and Android version so we
can reproduce the issue.
